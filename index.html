White-glove moving

The same three founders who restore the collection run every move — so anything valuable, fragile, or irreplaceable is handled like it's already ours.

I.

Private walkthrough

We assess in person and give a fixed quote — for a single heirloom piece or an entire estate.

II.

Custom crating

Fine pieces are individually padded, blanket-wrapped, and crated where needed — not stacked in a truck.

III.

Placement, not delivery

We unload, unwrap, and set every piece exactly where you want it, including anything new from the collection.

Three founders, one standard

Haul & Heir started when three friends — kept running into the same problem: good furniture, moved badly or thrown out due to a lack of space, ends up ruined.

We built the business backwards from that: source pieces worth keeping, then make sure the move never becomes the reason they don't survive another generation.
