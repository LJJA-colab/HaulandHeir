Three founders, one standard

The Heathwood House started with a simple frustration: most furniture today isn't built to last, and the pieces that are rarely get the attention they deserve.

We built the business around one idea: find genuinely well-made furniture, and hold it to a standard worth keeping for good — no shortcuts, no compromises.
